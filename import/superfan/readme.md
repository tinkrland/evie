# import/superfan — listening, reviews and collections

**medium:** music
**route:** mobile app (ios, android). export and api unverified.
**status:** designed pending route confirmation.

## what it is (verified 2026-11-04, from the app's own billing)

a social music dashboard: share current listens, review albums, track
concerts, log a vinyl collection, and get a weekly recap of listening
history. posts carry a numeric rating beside a short caption.

## why it is the interesting one for this vertical

most music sources are scrobble pipes: they know what played and nothing
else. superfan carries **both channels in one place**, because a review
post is a rating plus words. that makes it the first source that can
feed the verbal channel and the sonic channel from a single import,
which is exactly the separation this vertical exists to keep.

## what it must become

rows at track, album and artist level, each labelled with its channel:
listens are sonic, reviews are verbal plus sonic, and a numerical rating
alone is neither. a rating without words is a number, and numbers do not
become lyric evidence.

## the traps

1. **ratings are not the verbal channel.** interpreting a score as
   opinion about the writing would merge the channels by the back door.
2. **vinyl collection is acquisition, not listening.** owning a record
   is a fact about a shelf. useful context, never a play.
3. **concert tracking is attendance.** going to a show is an experience,
   not a statement about an album.
4. **weekly recaps are a display.** a recap screen is not an export.

## what to verify

whether account settings offer a data export, and whether reviews are
included in it. if the app will hand over listens but not reviews, the
folder imports sonic rows only and says so in the report.
